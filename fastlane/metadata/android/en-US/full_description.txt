Did someone already give the antibiotic this morning? Zero-Trust Meds lets parents, grandparents and babysitters share one record of every dose a child gets, with reminders, and keeps it end-to-end encrypted between you.

WHAT IT DOES
• A profile per child, shared with every caregiver by link or QR code.
• Admins manage medicines and treatments; caregivers see everything and log doses.
• Treatments at set times each day for a number of days, or "as needed" with a minimum gap and a daily most.
• Today's doses per child: Given, Due, Missed, Upcoming, Skipped or Paused, always with an icon and a label.
• One tap logs who gave a dose and when; change the time, skip with a reason, or undo.
• Warnings before a double dose, or an as-needed dose too soon or above the daily most.
• Reminders with a "Mark as given" button, a follow-up, a missed alert and quiet hours.
• Charts per medicine and per child over 7, 30 or 90 days, plus a family overview.
• Dose times follow the child's time zone, so trips and clock changes just work.
• Removing a caregiver changes the child's key, so they can't read anything new.

WHY ZERO-TRUST
• End-to-end encrypted: everything is encrypted on your phone before it syncs. We can't read it, and neither can anyone who breaks into the server.
• One private account for every Zero-Trust app, unlocked with your fingerprint or face.
• Works offline and syncs across your devices when you're back online.
• No ads, no trackers, no analytics, no data sold. Ever.
• Open source (AGPL-3.0), so anyone can check what the app does.
• English and Hebrew, with full right-to-left support.

Zero-Trust Meds helps you remember and record medicines. It does not give medical advice, check doses or check for interactions, and it is not a medical device. Always follow your doctor, your pharmacist and the label.

Part of the Zero-Trust suite: Notes, Tasks, Calendar, Contacts, Phone, Fitness, Meds, Time and Browser. One account opens them all.
